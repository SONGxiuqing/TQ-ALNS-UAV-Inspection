Data availability note

The manuscript reports 20 candidate platform sites and five opened platforms. The uploaded source data available for this package contain coordinates for the five selected/opened platforms, 95 monitoring points, five hazardous sources, three fixed no-fly zones, and the park boundary.

Coordinates for the other 15 candidate platform sites were not present in the supplied files. No synthetic coordinates were created to fill this gap. If the full 20-candidate-site file is later supplied, it should be added as `candidate_platforms_20.csv` and used for a complete location-selection audit.
